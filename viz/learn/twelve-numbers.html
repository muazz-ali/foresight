What the 12 numbers are

The model reads one list: 8 numbers about the hand, then 4 about the ball. This page names every slot. Same order as policy/features.py.

Hand: XYZ place, four turn numbers (w x y z), one gripper. Ball pack: future XY place, XY speed. Glue order is always robot first, ball second. Z of the ball, covariance, and “valid” stay out of this pack.
